Utility bills get processed, budgets get approved, and facilities teams stay busy putting out fires. But nobody is tracking whether the buildings are actually performing well, or what it would take to improve them. The result is unnecessary cost and emissions that compound quietly, year over year.

Before you ask

Straight answers.
Including the numbers.

01 What does the Energy Gap Analysis cost?

$5,000. It takes four to six weeks from data access to the presentation of findings.

We analyze one to two years of utility bills across your portfolio, benchmark each facility against its peers, and give you a specific figure for recoverable savings.

02 Will Rede be making changes to our building systems?

No. Rede never makes changes to your building automation system directly.

All corrections are made by your own controls contractor, with your authorization. We find the drift and document exactly what needs to change. Your team decides what gets implemented.

03 Do we need an energy manager on staff?

No.

RUN Intelligence assumes you have someone in-house who can interpret energy data. If you do not, RUN Insights adds two senior engineer sessions a year, and Energy Management puts Rede in that role, embedded alongside your team.

04 How much of our team's time does this take?

Less than most people expect. Recommissioning is a desktop-based activity and requires no site visits.

What we need is remote access to your building automation system, your mechanical and electrical drawings, and a controls lead who can implement the findings.

05 How long before we see savings?

Every recommissioning recommendation is selected for a two-year payback or less, sooner when incentives apply. RUN pays for itself faster: typical payback is two to five months.

Energy management is a longer horizon. One Western Canadian school division began working with Rede in 2016; that engagement has now accumulated $4.4M in savings.

06 What if our buildings are older, or we have no modern controls?

Recommissioning needs a modern building automation system with detailed controls coverage. Without it there is nothing to correct remotely.

If that is not your situation, the Gap Analysis is still the right starting point. It works from utility bills alone and tells you which buildings deserve attention first, whatever their age.

07 Are there incentives that reduce the cost?

Often, yes. Many provinces have programs that apply directly to recommissioning work.

Rede identifies and manages the incentive application process as part of every recommissioning engagement. You do not do the paperwork.

08 What does RUN cost, and what does it include?

RUN Intelligence is 1 to 2% of your annual energy spend, capped for public sector organizations. Typical payback is two to five months.

RUN Insights is Intelligence plus $20,000 a year, which adds two senior engineer sessions. Both include onboarding, monthly utility data updates, tutorials, and technical support.

09 Are we committed to anything after the Gap Analysis?

No lock-in. The Gap Analysis is a one-time diagnostic. What you do with the findings is your decision.

Most districts use it to build a case internally, whether or not the next step involves us.

10 Who do we call if something is not working?

Rede is a small team of engineers, which means you deal with the people doing the work rather than an account layer.

We have been doing this since 2008, in communities where the work had to be done well because there was no room for anything else.
