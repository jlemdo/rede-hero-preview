Energy Gap Analysis

Your buildings have a story to tell. Do you know what it is?

Most organizations are paying more for energy than they need to. The Gap Analysis tells you exactly how much, and where it is coming from.

Check your opportunity Book a call

What you receive

  • One to two years of utility data analyzed across your portfolio
  • Facility-level benchmarking against your peers
  • Identification of strategic priorities
  • A clear figure for recoverable savings
  • A presentation of findings with your team

What it is

A structured look at your utility data.

We collect one to two years of utility bills from across your portfolio and analyze them through RUN, our energy management platform. We benchmark your facilities against your peers, identify the highest-priority buildings, and calculate a clear figure for the energy costs you should not be paying.

The analysis takes roughly four to six weeks from data access to presentation. At the end of it, you will know exactly where your portfolio stands and what it would take to improve it.

What we have found

$2.94M

in unnecessary annual energy costs identified for a large Western Canadian school district.

These were costs that had been paid, year after year, without anyone knowing they were recoverable. The same diagnostic approach can apply to eligible commercial, institutional, municipal, post-secondary, and care facilities.
