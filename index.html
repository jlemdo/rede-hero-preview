The gap

Most organizations aren't under-managing their energy on purpose.

Facilities teams are stretched thin. Utility data is scattered. And when you're managing dozens of buildings, the gap between what you're paying and what you should be paying grows quietly, year over year.

  • Teams stretched thin

    Energy management falls to whoever has time, which means it does not get done properly. Teams stay reactive, responding to issues instead of preventing them.

  • Data scattered everywhere

    Utility data lives in accounts payable, arrives from multiple vendors in different formats, and takes real effort to consolidate before it tells you anything.

  • Costs compound quietly

    Without benchmarking, there is no way to know whether performance is good or bad. Budgets absorb the difference, and no one sees where the money is going.

Rede closes that gap.
