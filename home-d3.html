Common questions

Do we need a dedicated energy manager?

No. REDE is designed to add energy-management capacity without adding another full-time role to your team.

Can REDE work across different building types?

Yes. The portfolio approach creates a comparable view across schools, offices, recreation facilities and other multi-site public assets.

How quickly can we identify opportunities?

An initial portfolio screen can surface priority buildings quickly. Scope, eligibility and implementation timing are then confirmed with your team.

Is this only about capital projects?

No. REDE combines operational improvements, recommissioning, capital planning and ongoing measurement.
