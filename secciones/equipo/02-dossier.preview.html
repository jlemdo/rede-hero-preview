People behind Rede

More than engineers.

Rede is a small team of engineers, project managers, data analysts, and energy efficiency professionals. We work alongside your team rather than delivering a report and moving on.

  • Matthew Redekopp

    Founder and CEO

    P.Eng., CEM, LEED AP

    Matthew founded Rede in 2008 to serve communities and organizations that larger engineering firms were not reaching. Nearly two decades later, he leads a team helping organizations across Western Canada reduce energy costs and manage buildings more effectively.

  • Marco Bieri

    Energy Efficiency Engineer

    P.Eng., M.Eng., M.Sc., CEM

    Marco brings deep expertise in recommissioning, energy auditing, and building performance diagnostics, along with a rigorous approach to data analysis and measurement verification.

  • Erica Letchford

    Director of Operations

    BID, LEED AP

    Erica has spent nearly two decades working to reduce energy consumption and operating costs in residential, commercial, and institutional facilities. She brings a project manager's instinct for keeping the big picture in view while managing the details.
